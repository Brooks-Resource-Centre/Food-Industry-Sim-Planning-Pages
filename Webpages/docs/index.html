Plan an audit simulation scenario

Describe a food premises audit you would like students to practise. The development team builds it as a virtual environment that runs in a web browser on a PC and on a standalone VR headset.

  1. Fill in the form
    Plain language, no technical or game knowledge needed. Your answers save in your browser as you type.
  2. Download it as Word
    One click gives you a formatted document to review, share or send to the team.
  3. Hand it over
    The team uses it to build the scenario, then you try it with students.

What kind of scenario works well?

The best scenarios are ones where students look carefully, find problems and decide how serious they are. For example a torn door seal, droppings behind old stock, or a repair held together with tape. You can also point students to paperwork that confirms or explains what they find.

Each scenario lists 4 to 8 faults. For each one you say where it is, what the student sees, how serious it is under BRCGS and why it matters.
