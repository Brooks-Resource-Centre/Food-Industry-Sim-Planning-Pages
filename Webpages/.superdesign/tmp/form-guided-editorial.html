What is wrong? The faults to find

Aim for around 4 to 8. For each one say where it is, what the student would see, how serious it is, and why it matters.

How serious? BRCGS levels

  • Critical
    A failure on food safety or a legal issue (certificate refused or suspended).
  • Major
    A substantial failure to meet a requirement, or serious doubt about the product.
  • Minor
    A requirement is not fully met but the product is not in doubt.

What is BRCGS? Brand Reputation Compliance Global Standards (formerly the British Retail Consortium Global Standards). A widely used food safety certification scheme where an independent auditor inspects a site and grades problems as critical, major or minor.
