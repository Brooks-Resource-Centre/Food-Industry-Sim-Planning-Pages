Assets and resources

Every scenario is built from assets: the rooms, objects, marks, documents, people and sounds that make up the virtual premises. Planning your assets early tells the team what to build and how long it will take.

How it works

  1. You list what the scenario needs. In section 6 of the planning form, add each asset. The Suggest assets button fills in a first list from your faults and records.
  2. The team plans what can be shared. Assets common to several scenarios are built once and reused. Anything specific to your scenario is built new.
  3. You review. You check the finished assets look right and send reference material for anything that does not.

Planning tips

  • Every fault needs something the student can see. Each fault should have at least one asset, and each record is a document asset.
  • Fewer, richer assets are better. One dense room is quicker to build than several empty ones.
  • Reference material saves the most time. Collect photos and sample records early. In the form, mark each asset as Have photo, Have sample, Need help finding or Not needed.
  • Check permissions first. Photograph or model a real place only with permission, remove names and confidential details from real records, and check you have access to quote the BRCGS standard.
